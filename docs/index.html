The claim

A popular idea, tested without moving the goalposts

The method says that when pivots from monthly, weekly, and daily charts cluster into a “key level,” price will react there. Add a 4-hour trend filter and a 15-minute confirmation, and the win rate is said to be very high. We wrote the rules down before opening the answer, froze the code, and charged real costs.

“Key levels are high-probability reversal points.”
The strict hold rate was 15.5%. Price reached +1R before −1R in 49.2% of executed signals. That is close to a coin flip, not a high-probability reversal.
“Multi-timeframe confluence gives a 90% win rate.”
The sealed holdout win rate was 29.4% and the profit factor was 0.79. The 90% claim is not supported.
“The right settings make it work.”
18 of 18 pre-registered variants had a negative net Sharpe. The least-bad variant was still -2.26.
“Costs are a detail.”
At zero cost the holdout still lost −0.028R per trade. At the modelled cost it lost -0.166R. At three times the modelled cost it lost −0.442R. The sign never changes.
“The backtest proves it.”
The deflated Sharpe ratio is 1.14e-23 after 28 trials. The Reality Check and SPA p-values are 1.0000 and 1.0000. The random-entry placebo is not rejected (p = 0.7180).
“It beats the market.”
The holdout net Sharpe was -2.79. Buy-and-hold was +1.92 on the same window and cost model. The key-level rule was the worst of the five strategies tested.
Why this is useful even though it is negative. A negative result that is this well documented protects capital. It also leaves behind a reusable filter: pre-registration, a sealed holdout, realistic costs, benchmarks, placebos, and multiple-testing controls. Use it on the next idea before you fund it.

What was tested

The rules, in plain English

This is one explicit, mechanical interpretation of a discretionary idea. The full specification is in the pre-registration document.

1

Find the levels

Monthly, weekly, and daily pivots are clustered into levels. Pivots within 0.50 × ATR(D1) become one level. A level needs at least two touches.

2

Filter the direction

The 4-hour trend is read from the last two confirmed swing highs and lows. Only trades in the trend direction are allowed.

3

Wait for the trigger

On the 15-minute chart, price must touch the level and close back through it. Two confirmation closes and a directional candle are required. The entry is the next bar open.

4

Manage the risk

The stop sits just beyond the level or the 5-bar extreme, capped at 4 × ATR. The target is the next opposite level, default 2R, capped at 5R. The time stop is 96 bars.

cd keylevel-mtf-research
python -m venv .venv
source .venv/bin/activate
pip install -r requirements.txt

The method you can reuse

  • Write the rules and success criteria before the test.
  • Freeze the code and hash it.
  • Keep a sealed holdout and open it once.
  • Charge realistic costs on both sides.
  • Compare with simple benchmarks.
  • Compare with random entries and random levels.
  • Correct for the number of trials.
  • Publish the negative result, not just the winner.
